Event Overview & Mandate

A Premier Showcase of Karnataka's Young Innovators

The Young Entrepreneurs Summit (YES 2026) is the flagship annual gathering of VTU’s Visvesvaraya Research and Innovation Foundation (VRIF). The Summit brings together beneficiaries, stakeholders, mentors, incubatees, industry leaders, and the VRIF executive team on a single platform for celebration, engagement, networking, and strategic partnership building.

Strategic Milestones

Catalyzing Research into Enterprise

The Summit showcases the pivotal work carried out by VRIF over the past year and marks key strategic milestones, including the formal inauguration of VRIF Technology Business Incubator (TBI) infrastructure, the grand onboarding of Navodaya Cohort 1, and celebrations of milestones achieved through SamShoDhana, SheInnovates, and the Vinyasa Program.

Incubation Infrastructure Seed Grants & Mentorship Patenting & Tech Transfer

Incubator Initiatives

VRIF Innovation & Incubation Umbrella

Empowering research, enterprise, and inclusive startup leadership from Karnataka to the world.

TBI Cohort 1

Navodaya Incubation Program

VRIF's flagship Technology Business Incubator induction track offering selected startups physical lab space, seed funding access, legal compliance backing, and dedicated mentor desks on the 2nd Floor of the VRIF Building.

Research Translation

SamShoDhana Initiative

Dedicated vehicle for translating academic laboratory findings, engineering patents, and doctoral thesis breakthroughs into commercially viable, market-ready deep-tech products.

SheInnovates Program

Flagship platform committed to funding, mentoring, and accelerating women-founded technology enterprises across North Karnataka and VTU-affiliated institutions.

Intensive market-readiness sprint designed to sharpen investor pitches, scale product-market fit, and connect high-growth ventures directly with venture capital syndicates.
